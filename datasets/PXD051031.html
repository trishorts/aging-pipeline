isobaric_unlabelled_peptides warning
8 row(s) of PeptideQuantification.tsv carry no multiplex label, with 56 value(s), stored as written. A peptide with no label gives no reporter ions of its own, so these values are co-isolated interference (QuantProject 066, rule 4); a protein group's value can include them.
